Local to Beaumont

Carpet cleaning built around Beaumont's homes

Beaumont is best known for Beaumont Hospital, one of Dublin's major hospitals, sitting at the heart of a settled suburb of mostly 1960s and 1970s semi-detached housing on the northside. It's a stable, family-oriented area, but the hospital shapes daily life here in a way that's genuinely different from a standard commuter suburb.

A meaningful share of households in Beaumont work hospital shifts — nights, earlies, irregular weekends — rather than a conventional nine-to-five week. That's a real, practical difference when it comes to booking a home visit: fitting an appointment around a shift pattern actually matters here, more than it would in a suburb where everyone's roughly free after 6pm on weekdays.
