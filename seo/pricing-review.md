## Trustworthiness assessment (why these figures are NOT going on the new site)

- The table is **internally consistent** — a worked "3-bed house = €205" example in the site's own FAQ matches the per-room prices exactly (3×€35 + €50 + €50 = €205). This suggests it was a deliberately calculated price list at some point, not random numbers.
- However, it lives only on the **legacy "Spotless" homepage content block**, which sits alongside a footer copyright of "© 2024" and blog posts dated March 2023. The rest of the live site (service pages, about, contact) has been rebranded to "Krystal Klean Express" with a different phone number and email, and the pricing table shows no sign of being touched during that rebrand.
- It is now **2026** — these figures are plausibly **2–3+ years old** with no visible revision since. Irish wage/cost inflation over that period means these numbers cannot be assumed current.
- No "last updated" date exists anywhere near the table.

## Recommendation

**Do not publish any of these euro figures as current pricing on carpetcleaningservice.ie.** This is consistent with the approach `carpet-cleaning-prices-dublin/` already takes (cost-factors explanation + free quote, no published numbers) — nothing changes there as a result of this audit.

**What IS reusable:** the **pricing structure/model** — per-room for carpets, per-seat for upholstery, per-size for mattresses — is a sound, customer-friendly way to think about a future price list, once the owner has current, deliberately-checked figures to publish. This is noted in `content-strategy.md`'s "future content" section as the recommended shape for a real price list when one is ready, not a reason to publish today's guesswork.

**If the owner wants to publish real prices**, the fastest legitimate path is: confirm current 2026 figures directly (not by trusting the 2023-era table), then either update `carpet-cleaning-prices-dublin/` with an itemized table (matching what the competitor research identified as the single biggest differentiator against Dublin competitors who all hide pricing) or ask me to build it once confirmed numbers are provided.
