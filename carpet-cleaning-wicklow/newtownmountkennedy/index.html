Carpet cleaning built around Newtownmountkennedy's homes

Newtownmountkennedy sits directly on the N11 between Ashford and Kilpedder, and has seen a wave of new housing development in recent years that has considerably expanded the village well beyond its original core, growing into one of the busier commuter spots in north Wicklow.
