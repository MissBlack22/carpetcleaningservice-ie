Local to Knocklyon

Carpet cleaning built around Knocklyon's homes

Knocklyon is a suburban residential area in South Dublin, developed mainly from the 1970s and 80s, sitting close to Templeogue, Firhouse and Rathfarnham near the edge of the Dublin Mountains foothills. It's a family-oriented suburb of mostly semi-detached and bungalow-style housing, built up around local schools, shops and green spaces.

With most of the housing dating from a similar era, a lot of the carpet we clean in Knocklyon has been down for years in settled family homes, and being close to the mountain foothills means gardens and green areas nearby occasionally bring extra mud and debris onto carpets and hallways.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Knocklyon

Do you cover Templeogue and Firhouse as well as Knocklyon?

Yes — Templeogue, Firhouse and Rathfarnham are all close by and we treat this part of South Dublin as one patch. If you're not sure whether your road is covered, get in touch and we'll confirm.

Is an original carpet in a 1970s or 80s Knocklyon home worth cleaning, or should it be replaced?

It depends on the fibre and condition, which is why we check before recommending anything. Plenty of the carpet we clean in Knocklyon's semis and bungalows has been in place for decades and responds well to a proper clean, but where wear has genuinely gone too far we'll say so honestly.
