Phibsborough

A northside inner suburb built around the Mater and the canal

Phibsborough is a high-density northside inner suburb of red-brick Victorian terraces, sitting along the Royal Canal within easy reach of the Mater Hospital and Croke Park, and served by the Luas. Like Drumcondra just across the way, it's an established rental hotspot — a large share of the terraced housing stock has been split into flats and let to students and young professionals cycling through on short leases.

What sets Phibsborough apart is the Mater Hospital next door. A meaningful slice of the local renter population works hospital shifts rather than a standard office day, which changes not just what needs cleaning but when — booking around an early, a late, or a day off mid-rotation, rather than assuming everyone's free after 6pm on a weekday.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Phibsborough

Do you offer flexible booking for hospital shift workers renting in Phibsborough?

Yes. With the Mater Hospital right on Phibsborough's doorstep, a good number of the tenants and homeowners we deal with here work shifts rather than a standard nine-to-five, so we try to offer appointment times that fit around that — early morning, evening or the day off in the middle of a shift rotation — rather than only office-hours slots.

Can you clean a carpet before new tenants move in, to a fixed handover date?

Yes, that's one of the most common jobs we do in Phibsborough. Tell us the date the property needs to be ready and we'll work back from it. See our dedicated end of tenancy carpet cleaning Phibsborough page for how we handle handover-date bookings specifically in this area.
