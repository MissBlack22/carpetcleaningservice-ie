# Content Strategy — carpetcleaningservice.ie

## Priority order (commercial intent first, per brief)

1. **Dublin-wide commercial service pillars** (carpet, upholstery/sofa, mattress, rug, stain, pet-stain, commercial, end-of-tenancy) — highest, most direct commercial intent. Built first.
2. **Location pages** — `[service] [area]` long-tail commercial intent, proven by competitor presence across nearly every Dublin suburb (`competitor-research.md` §6). 44 built (26 Tier 1 + 18 Tier 2), tiered by reasoned demand (`keyword-research-raw.md` §2), not built as flat 58-page spam.
3. **End-of-tenancy location spin-offs** — the one service×location combination with genuinely distinct persona/intent, kept to 5 areas rather than all 58 (`keyword-research-raw.md` §3).
4. **Pricing/FAQ hub** — high commercial-investigative intent, proven as a real, separate traffic channel that smaller blog-style competitor pages currently win (`competitor-research.md` §6) — built as a proper page, not a blog post.
5. **Guides (informational)** — deliberately last and deliberately smaller in number (14, not 40+). Each guide exists because it feeds a specific commercial page and answers a real, distinct search question — not to inflate word count or page count. Topics that would only pad the site (carpet-fibre history, generic "types of carpet" listicles) were rejected during keyword research.

## Why this ordering

Per the brief: "Commercial content first. Random informational traffic doesn't pay the bills." The site's structure reflects that literally — of 79 total pages, 10 are Dublin-wide commercial pillars, 44 are commercial location pages, 5 are commercial EOT spin-offs (59 commercial pages), 14 are informational guides, and 6 are navigational/hub/legal pages (home, locations hub, guides hub, about, contact, privacy).

## How guides support commercial pages (never orphaned)

Every guide links to at least one commercial page relevant to its topic (see `keyword-map.md` §D). Every commercial pillar page links to at least one guide. The guides hub (`/guides/`) links to all 14; nothing is an orphan.

## Future content (not built in this pass — flagged for later, once real data exists)

- **Real before/after photo pairs** per location page, once genuine project photography exists — `competitor-research.md` §7 flags this as unclaimed ground (no competitor pairs real before/after proof with a specific suburb page).
- **Bundle/combo pricing** (e.g. carpet + upholstery + mattress in one visit) as a named offer — currently not built because it requires real pricing, which we don't have; the pricing page is architected so a bundle section can be added later without restructuring.
- **Promote Tier 3 areas to their own page** if Search Console data (once live) shows independent search demand for one of the 14 folded-in areas — `keyword-research-raw.md` §2 recommends revisiting this after 3–6 months of real traffic data, not guessing now.
- **Postal-district content** (D2, D4, D6, D15 etc.) — deliberately NOT built as separate pages, only as secondary on-page/schema mentions, to avoid future cannibalization against the neighbourhood-name pages (`keyword-research-raw.md` §5, point 6).
