Local to Dolphin's Barn

Carpet cleaning built around Dolphin's Barn's homes

Dolphin's Barn sits on the Grand Canal, in the stretch between the Liberties and Crumlin — and its housing reflects both neighbours rather than one distinct style of its own. There's older terraced housing with the same inner-city character found in the Liberties nearby, alongside social housing in the same tradition as Crumlin's classic Corporation-era streets a little further out. It's a genuine connector area, and we treat it as one rather than forcing a single identity onto it.

Most of what we're asked to clean is straightforward whole-house work in family homes that have been lived in for years — carpets and stair runners due proper care rather than replacement, plus the general stains and everyday marks that come with any long-lived-in house.
