Local to Rathcoole

Carpet cleaning built around Rathcoole's homes

Rathcoole is a long-established village on the old road out of Dublin towards Naas and Cork, now bypassed by the M7 but still very much a working village in its own right, with newer estate housing built up around it in recent decades. That gives us a genuine mix to work with — older village-core homes alongside more recent estates.

It sits at the edge of where suburban Dublin gives way to the countryside towards Newcastle and Brittas, so alongside typical estate housing we also see more rural, one-off properties on the outskirts of the village, each with its own access and layout to work around.
