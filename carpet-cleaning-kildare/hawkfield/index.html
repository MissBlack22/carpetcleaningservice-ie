FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Hawkfield

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Hawkfield?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and access — we don't quote a flat rate without the details. See our carpet cleaning prices page, or request a free quote above for a clear price before booking.

Do you cover Naas and Kilcullen as well as Hawkfield?

Yes — Hawkfield sits right on the road between Naas and Kilcullen, and we cover all three as part of the same area. If you're nearby but not sure your address counts, just get in touch and we'll confirm.

Do you clean carpets on site or take them away?

Carpets and fitted flooring are always cleaned in place, in your home. Rugs can usually be cleaned on site too, but for delicate or heavily soiled pieces we may recommend collecting them for a more thorough clean — we'll discuss the best option before starting.

What's included in a typical clean?

A visit covers an assessment of the carpet, pre-treatment of stains, and a full hot water extraction clean of the agreed rooms, with a final check before we leave. Mattress or upholstery cleaning can be added to the same visit — just mention it when you request your quote.
