Local to Oldbawn

Carpet cleaning built around Oldbawn's homes

Oldbawn is one of the older residential parts of Tallaght, built up along the River Dodder well before much of the newer development further out towards Citywest and Saggart. It's a settled, mostly family suburb of semi-detached and terraced housing, with the kind of long-term occupancy that means a lot of the carpet we're called to clean has been down for years rather than a few seasons.

Being this established also means we see a genuine mix of carpet ages and conditions across Oldbawn — some homes have been refreshed regularly, others have original carpet that's simply been well looked after. We check condition honestly rather than assuming either.

Why it matters here

Why professional cleaning matters in Oldbawn specifically

Long-established housing

Oldbawn's estates were built up well before much of Tallaght's later expansion, so a fair amount of the carpet we clean here has been down for many years.

Family homes, not turnover rentals

Most of the work here is whole-house cleaning for owner-occupied family homes rather than the quick-turnaround work common in rental-heavy areas.

Riverside setting

With the Dodder running through the area, some gardens and ground-floor rooms see more humidity than others, which we factor into drying times.

Genuine Tallaght coverage

We treat Oldbawn as part of the same patch as Firhouse, Templeogue and the rest of Tallaght, not a separate fringe job.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Oldbawn

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Oldbawn?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and any furniture or access considerations — there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for a full breakdown, or request a free quote and we'll come back with a clear price before booking anything.

Is older carpet in a long-lived-in Oldbawn home worth cleaning or replacing?

It depends on the fibre and condition, which is why we check before recommending anything. A lot of the carpet we see in Oldbawn's established homes has been well looked after and responds well to a proper clean — where wear has genuinely gone too far, we'll say so rather than take the job anyway.

Do you cover Firhouse and Templeogue as well as Oldbawn?

Yes — Firhouse and Templeogue are right next to Oldbawn and we treat the whole area as one patch, along with the rest of Tallaght. If you're not sure whether your road is covered, get in touch and we'll confirm.

Can you treat pet stains and odours as well as general carpet cleaning?

Yes — pet stains and odours are a routine part of the job in family homes like the ones around Oldbawn. We treat the source of the mark and odour rather than just cleaning over the top of it. See pet stain & odour removal.
