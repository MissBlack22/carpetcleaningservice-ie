Local to Avoca

Carpet cleaning built around Avoca's homes

Avoca sits in the wooded Vale of Avoca, at the point where the Avonmore and Avonbeg rivers join at the Meeting of the Waters, a spot celebrated in Thomas Moore's well-known poem and still one of the more recognisable beauty spots in the county. The village itself is home to the original Avoca Handweavers mill, one of Ireland's oldest working woollen mills, and its picturesque main street was used as a filming location for the RTÉ/BBC series Ballykissangel through the 1990s, which has kept a steady trickle of visitors coming ever since.

That visitor trade sits alongside the everyday business of a small Wicklow village — period stone cottages and terraces along the valley, a handful of guesthouses and B&Bs serving tourism, and newer homes on the outskirts. Wool has a real history here too, so we're used to checking fibre carefully before cleaning any wool carpet or rug, which needs a gentler approach than a synthetic fitted carpet.
