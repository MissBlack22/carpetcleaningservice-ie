Carpet cleaning built around Clara's homes

Clara lies in the wooded Vale of Clara, on the Avonmore River along the road between Rathdrum and Laragh towards Glendalough — a stretch of the county familiar to a lot of Wicklow families thanks to Clara-Lara Fun Park on the riverbank nearby. Away from that, it's a quiet rural area of scattered homes and cottages rather than a dense village centre.

Most of what we clean around Clara is carpet in homes spread along the valley road rather than a concentrated cluster of streets, so we're used to travelling out to individual houses across the area. Older cottages sit alongside newer builds, and we check each carpet's condition and fibre before recommending how best to clean it.
