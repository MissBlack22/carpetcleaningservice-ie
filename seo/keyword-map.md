## E. Cannibalization guardrails (enforced across this map)

1. `/upholstery-cleaning-dublin/` owns both "upholstery" and "sofa" head terms — no separate `/sofa-cleaning-dublin/` exists.
2. Location pages target `[service] [area]` long-tail only; Dublin-wide pillars own the `[service] dublin` head terms. Location-page service sections must not repeat pillar-page copy verbatim.
3. EOT location spin-offs target the landlord/tenant persona only; the parent location page keeps one link to its spin-off, not duplicate tenancy content.
4. `/carpet-cleaning-prices-dublin/` is the only page allowed a full pricing-factors breakdown; all other pages link to it instead of repeating it.
5. Tier 3 areas get a substantive paragraph on exactly one parent page + a one-line mention on `/locations/` — never both in full.
