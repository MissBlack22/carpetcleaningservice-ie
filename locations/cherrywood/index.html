Local to Cherrywood

Carpet cleaning built around Cherrywood's newer homes

Cherrywood is one of South Dublin's newer large-scale developments, in D18, built up mainly from the 2000s onward around Cherrywood Business Park. It's served by its own stop on the Luas Green Line, and combines modern apartment blocks and housing with retail and office space, sitting near Loughlinstown and Shankill with the foothills of the Dublin Mountains close by to the west.

Because so much of Cherrywood's housing is recent, the carpet we're asked to clean here tends to be newer fitted carpet rather than decades-old original flooring, and a lot of it is in apartment blocks rather than houses with their own driveway or garden. That changes some practical things about a job — building access, lift use and parking are usually more relevant here than in an older suburb — so we plan around that rather than assuming every visit works the same way.

Cherrywood is mostly modern apartments — do you clean carpet in apartment blocks?

Yes. A lot of Cherrywood's housing is newer apartment blocks built as part of the development, and we're used to working around building access, lifts and parking rather than a house with its own driveway. Just let us know the building details when you get in touch so we can plan the visit properly.

Cherrywood is a newer, Luas-served development — does that affect the carpet you clean there?

It does, a little. Since Cherrywood has grown mainly from the 2000s onward around the business park and its own Luas Green Line stop, most of the carpet we see is newer fitted carpet rather than decades-old original flooring, so the work is usually more about a thorough clean than assessing very old fibre.
