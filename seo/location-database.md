### Terenure — BENCHMARK PAGE

**LOCATION:** Southside suburb, south of Rathgar/Harold's Cross, on the south-central residential belt of the city.

**AREA TYPE:** Established, family-oriented southside suburb with a village-scale local centre.

**PROPERTY TYPES:** Red-brick Victorian/Edwardian terraces and bay-window houses close to the village core; a large stock of 1930s–1950s bay-window semi-detached and detached family homes on tree-lined roads further out; some later infill and low-rise apartment blocks along the main roads.

**LOCAL CHARACTER:** Quiet, settled, "villagey" southside suburb with a strong owner-occupier base and long tenures — noticeably less transient than the canal-belt suburbs closer to the city (Rathmines, Portobello). Good schools and a stable local centre make it a draw for families staying put for years.

**NEARBY AREAS:** Rathgar, Rathmines, Kimmage, Harold's Cross, Crumlin.

**LIKELY CUSTOMER TYPES:** Long-term homeowner families with children and pets; older residents in the same family home for decades; a smaller landlord segment renting out subdivided older houses.

**LIKELY CLEANING NEEDS:** Whole-house carpet cleaning driven by kids/pets/muddy shoes rather than tenancy turnover; stair-runner and landing carpet cleaning in older bay-window houses; upholstery cleaning for long-owned three-piece suites; seasonal (post-Christmas, spring) bookings.

**UNIQUE CONTENT ANGLES:** Terenure is the clearest "long-term homeowner, not high-turnover renter" area on the list — the pitch is protecting an investment you've owned for 15–20 years, not getting a deposit back, which is a genuinely different angle from the canal-belt Tier 1 pages nearby. Larger gardens and more houses-with-gardens (vs. flats) than Rathmines/Portobello also means pet ownership is more common here, giving pet-stain content real local relevance rather than being generic.

**SERVICE OPPORTUNITIES:** Pet stain/odour removal (garden houses + pets), whole-house carpet packages, rug cleaning (established area, older/better rugs likely present), upholstery cleaning for period three-piece suites.

**FAQ OPPORTUNITIES:** "We've had the same carpet for 15+ years — can it still be professionally cleaned or is it too late?" / "Do you clean carpets in homes with dogs or cats?" / "How do you protect wooden floors and skirting boards when cleaning stair carpets in an older bay-window house?"

**INTERNAL LINK OPPORTUNITIES:** Rathgar, Rathmines, Kimmage, Harold's Cross, Crumlin location pages; pet-stain-removal-dublin pillar; carpet-cleaning-dublin pillar; rug-cleaning-dublin pillar.

### Ballsbridge — BENCHMARK PAGE

**LOCATION:** Affluent southside area close to the Grand Canal and Dublin Bay, one of Dublin's best-known addresses.

**AREA TYPE:** Affluent, semi-institutional southside area — part residential, part embassy/office district.

**PROPERTY TYPES:** Large Victorian/Edwardian red-brick houses, a meaningful share of which are now embassies, offices, or converted into high-end apartments; modern redeveloped apartment blocks; some hotel/serviced-apartment stock.

**LOCAL CHARACTER:** Dublin's diplomatic quarter — home to a notable concentration of embassies and international organisations — close to Aviva Stadium and Herbert Park. Less "villagey" than Ranelagh or Donnybrook; more institutional and corporate in character, with a genuinely high-end lettings market (corporate and embassy-linked tenancies).

**NEARBY AREAS:** Donnybrook, Sandymount, Ringsend, Beggars Bush (Tier 3), Merrion (Tier 3).

**LIKELY CUSTOMER TYPES:** Corporate/embassy-linked tenants and their landlords, high-end residential landlords, hotel/serviced-apartment operators, affluent owner-occupiers, office/embassy facilities managers.

**LIKELY CLEANING NEEDS:** High-spec residential cleaning between corporate lets; commercial carpet cleaning for offices and embassies (distinct proof points — insurance, discretion, out-of-hours access); upholstery cleaning for reception/boardroom furniture; occasional short-notice cleans around Aviva Stadium event weekends for nearby short-let apartments.

**UNIQUE CONTENT ANGLES:** Ballsbridge is the one Tier 1 benchmark area where a genuine commercial/institutional buyer exists alongside the residential one — unlike the other four benchmarks, it can legitimately cross-link into the commercial-carpet-cleaning pillar with area-specific framing (discretion, out-of-hours, insurance for embassies/offices) without becoming a standalone "commercial + neighbourhood" page, which the keyword research explicitly ruled out as unjustified elsewhere.

**SERVICE OPPORTUNITIES:** Commercial carpet cleaning (office/embassy section with area-specific framing), high-end residential end-of-tenancy for corporate lets, upholstery cleaning for offices and formal reception areas.

**FAQ OPPORTUNITIES:** "Do you clean carpets for offices and embassies in Ballsbridge, not just private homes?" / "Can you work out-of-hours or with added discretion for a business or embassy?" / "Do you offer short-notice cleans around Aviva Stadium event weekends for nearby short-let apartments?"

**INTERNAL LINK OPPORTUNITIES:** Donnybrook, Sandymount, Ringsend location pages; commercial-carpet-cleaning-dublin pillar; end-of-tenancy-carpet-cleaning-dublin pillar (general — no dedicated spin-off page exists for this area).

### Clontarf — BENCHMARK PAGE

**LOCATION:** Northside coastal suburb along Dublin Bay, north-east of the city centre.

**AREA TYPE:** Affluent northside seafront suburb.

**PROPERTY TYPES:** Large period red-brick and Victorian homes along the coast road and main roads; mid-20th-century semi-detached suburban housing further back from the coast; modern apartment developments along the seafront.

**LOCAL CHARACTER:** Established, family-oriented seafront suburb with a strong sense of place — the coast road, Dublin Bay views, Bull Island/Dollymount Strand nature reserve and St Anne's Park nearby, DART line running along the coast. A genuine draw for long-tenure homeowner families and, along the front, for apartment owners wanting sea views.

**NEARBY AREAS:** Fairview, Marino, Raheny, Killester, Dollymount (Tier 3).

**LIKELY CUSTOMER TYPES:** Long-established homeowner families, some coastal-apartment owners/renters, dog-owning households (the strand and St Anne's Park are popular dog-walking spots).

**LIKELY CLEANING NEEDS:** Whole-house carpet cleaning for family homes; pet-related stain/odour removal; rug cleaning for period homes; upholstery/carpet care that accounts for the coastal micro-climate.

**UNIQUE CONTENT ANGLES:** A genuinely distinct soiling and ageing profile from inland suburbs — sea-air humidity and sand/grit tracked in from the strand and from dog walks affect carpets and soft furnishings differently near the coast. This is a real, well-understood general effect of coastal living, not a fabricated claim, and gives Clontarf a legitimate content angle (frequency of cleaning, salt/sand removal) that doesn't apply to most of the other 57 areas.

**SERVICE OPPORTUNITIES:** Pet stain/odour removal (dog-walking suburb), rug cleaning for period homes, a cross-link into the damp/mould informational guide (coastal humidity is a real, honest tie-in).

**FAQ OPPORTUNITIES:** "Does sea air or coastal humidity affect how often carpets need professional cleaning near the coast?" / "Can you remove sand and salt residue tracked in from Dollymount Strand or dog walks?" / "Do you clean carpets in seafront apartments as well as houses?"

### Rathmines — BENCHMARK PAGE

**LOCATION:** Dense southside inner suburb just south of the Grand Canal, one of Dublin's most recognisable neighbourhood names.

**AREA TYPE:** High-density southside inner suburb and major local hub.

**PROPERTY TYPES:** Red-brick Victorian/Edwardian terraces, a large share converted into flats/apartments/bedsits; some larger period houses remain single-family; newer apartment blocks along the main roads.

**LOCAL CHARACTER:** Extremely high rental density (students and young professionals dominate), a busy village-scale main street, and a genuinely transient population. Housing stock is a real mix — hard flooring in many converted flats, carpeted stairs and landings in period buildings still common.

**NEARBY AREAS:** Rathgar, Ranelagh, Portobello, Harold's Cross, Terenure, Dartry (Tier 3).

**LIKELY CUSTOMER TYPES:** Landlords and letting agents managing multiple converted-flat units, young professional and student tenants, some longer-term family owner-occupiers on quieter side streets.

**LIKELY CLEANING NEEDS:** End-of-tenancy carpet cleaning at real scale (already the strongest end-of-tenancy market on the list); shared/communal stair carpet cleaning in subdivided houses; small-footprint apartment carpet and rug cleaning; fast turnaround between tenancies.

**UNIQUE CONTENT ANGLES:** This is the flagship high-turnover rental market on the whole list — the differentiating content is the landlord/letting-agent relationship, the physically distinct job of cleaning shared stair carpets in a subdivided house (genuinely different work from a single-family home), and fast turnaround against letting deadlines. Live SERP research already confirmed Rathmines is the pattern every other Tier 1 page is being benchmarked against.

**SERVICE OPPORTUNITIES:** End-of-tenancy carpet cleaning (dedicated spin-off page exists), landlord multi-unit service arrangements, same-day/fast-turnaround service for letting deadlines.

**FAQ OPPORTUNITIES:** "Can you turn a carpet clean around the same day before new tenants move in?" / "Do you offer arrangements for landlords with several rental properties in Rathmines?" / "Our house is split into flats with a shared stair carpet — do you clean communal areas?"

**INTERNAL LINK OPPORTUNITIES:** Rathgar, Ranelagh, Portobello, Harold's Cross, Terenure location pages; end-of-tenancy-carpet-cleaning-rathmines spin-off page; end-of-tenancy-carpet-cleaning-dublin pillar.
