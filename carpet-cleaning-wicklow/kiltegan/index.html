FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Kiltegan

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Kiltegan?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and access to the property, so there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for what affects cost, or request a free quote above and we'll come back with a clear price before booking anything.

Do you cover Kiltegan as well as Baltinglass?

Yes — Kiltegan is part of the same south-west Wicklow area we cover alongside Baltinglass and the surrounding townlands. If you're not sure whether your road is included, get in touch and we'll confirm.

Do you clean carpets on site, or do items need to be collected?

Fitted carpet is always cleaned in place. Rugs can often be cleaned in place too, but depending on condition and construction we may suggest collecting one for off-site cleaning instead — we'll agree the right approach before starting.

What does a typical carpet cleaning visit include?

We check the carpet and agree what's being cleaned, pre-treat any marked or heavily-used areas, then carry out a full hot water extraction clean and give advice on drying afterwards. The exact scope depends on the job, confirmed when quoting.
