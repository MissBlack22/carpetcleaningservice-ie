Local to Kilberry

Carpet cleaning built around Kilberry

Kilberry is a small village a short drive from Athy in south County Kildare, set among farmland and quiet rural roads. It's a village in the traditional sense — a church, a crossroads and houses gathered around it — rather than a commercial centre.

We cover Kilberry as part of the wider Athy area, alongside Ballitore and Narraghmore. As with much of rural south Kildare, homes here can mean a longer driveway or a farmhouse set back from the road, which we're happy to work around.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Kilberry

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Kilberry?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and access — there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for what affects cost, or request a free quote above.

Do you cover Athy as well as Kilberry?

Yes — Kilberry is close to Athy and we cover the two as part of the same south Kildare patch, along with Ballitore and Narraghmore nearby. Get in touch if you're not sure your exact address is included.

Do you clean rugs on site or collect them?

Most rugs can be cleaned in place, on the floor where they sit. For delicate or heavily soiled rugs we may suggest collecting them for a more thorough off-site clean — we'll agree the right approach with you before we start.

What's included in a carpet cleaning visit?

A visit covers an assessment of the carpet, pre-treatment of any stains, and a full hot water extraction clean of the agreed rooms, followed by a check before we leave. Mattress or upholstery cleaning can be added to the same visit if you'd like.
