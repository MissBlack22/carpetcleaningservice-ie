Local to Broadstone

Carpet cleaning built around Broadstone's mix of old and new

Broadstone takes its name from the historic Broadstone railway terminus building in Dublin 7, a Victorian-era structure that later served as a bus garage and now sits alongside the Broadstone – DIT Luas stop on the Green Line, brought into the city centre as part of the Luas Cross City extension. The TU Dublin Grangegorman campus sits right beside it, which has reshaped a lot of the immediate area with newer student and residential apartment blocks.

That combination — a genuinely old inner-city area with a large, relatively recent college campus dropped into the middle of it — means the carpet work here is more varied than in a single-era suburb. We're called out to older converted buildings around Constitution Hill and the surrounding streets just as often as newer apartment developments built to serve the campus and the Luas line, and each needs its own read before we start.

Why it matters here

Why professional cleaning matters in Broadstone specifically

A mix of old and new buildings

Older Victorian and Georgian-era buildings sit close to newer apartment blocks near the TU Dublin campus, and each type of building calls for a different approach to carpet and flooring care.

Apartment access and building management

A lot of the work near the Luas stop and college campus is in apartment blocks, so we're used to working around building access, lifts and management arrangements.

High footfall, inner-city living

Being close to a Luas stop and a large campus means more daily foot traffic through hallways and living spaces than a quieter suburb, which shows up faster in carpet wear.

Compact inner-city rooms

Inner-city floor plans, whether in an older conversion or a newer block, often mean tighter room layouts and more furniture to work around than a typical suburban semi.
