FAQ

Common questions

How does it work, from quote to finished job?

Get in touch by phone or through the quote form with a few details about the job — number of rooms, general condition, anything unusual. We'll come back with a free, no-obligation price, and if you're happy to go ahead we'll arrange a time. Payment isn't due until the work is done.

Is the quote really free, with no obligation to book?

Yes — there's no charge for a quote and no obligation to book once you have one. If the price isn't right for you, or you just want to think it over, that's completely fine. No call-out fee, no pressure.

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Dublin?

Price depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition, and any furniture that needs moving. See our carpet cleaning prices guide for a full breakdown of what affects cost, or request a free quote above.

What payment methods do you accept, and when do I pay?

We accept common methods like card and bank transfer — just confirm what suits when you're booking. Payment is taken once the job is finished, not upfront.

Do you cover my area of Dublin?

We cover Dublin city and county. Check our areas we cover page, or just get in touch — if you're in Dublin, we can very likely help.

Can you fit me in quickly, or is there a long wait for a booking?

It varies with demand and time of year. Get in touch as early as you can, especially around a house move or an end-of-tenancy deadline, and we'll give you the soonest realistic date rather than a promise we can't keep.

Do I need to be at home during the clean?

It's up to you. Once we can get access and you've told us what needs doing, plenty of customers head out and let us get on with it. If you'd rather be around, especially the first time, that's no problem — just let us know when you book.

What's included in a visit, and what might cost extra?

A standard visit covers cleaning whatever you've booked in for. Things like stairs, heavily soiled carpets or furniture that needs moving can add time and may affect the price — mention these when you request your quote so the figure reflects the actual job. See our prices guide for the main factors.

How long does carpet take to dry after cleaning?

It depends on the carpet, the level of soiling and ventilation in the room. We'll give you a realistic drying estimate before we start, and tips to speed it up. See our guides for more.

What happens if a stain or mark doesn't fully come out?

We'll always be upfront with you. Some stains, particularly old or set-in ones, don't lift completely, and we'd rather tell you that honestly before we start than promise a result we can't stand over. See our stain removal page for more on what affects the outcome.

Do you clean anything besides carpets?

Yes — we also clean upholstery and sofas, mattresses and rugs, alongside carpets. Just mention what you need when you request a quote.

Can you clean carpets for a rental property before handover?

Yes — see our end of tenancy carpet cleaning page for how we work with tenants, landlords and letting agents.
