Local to Chapelizod

Carpet cleaning built around Chapelizod's village

Chapelizod is a small, historic riverside village in Dublin 20, sitting on the River Liffey right by the Chapelizod Gate entrance to Phoenix Park. Its main street still carries an old village core of stone cottages and terraced houses, with later housing built up around that centre. Local tradition even links the village's name to a "Chapel of Iseult" connected with the old Tristan and Iseult legend — a nice piece of folklore rather than a documented fact, but part of what makes the village distinctive. Neighbouring areas include Palmerstown, Islandbridge, Kilmainham and Inchicore.

That older village core means a fair amount of what we clean in Chapelizod is carpet in stone cottages and terraced houses that have stood for a long time, often with tighter rooms, narrower stairs and older underlay than you'd find in a modern estate. Riverside and park-side settings also bring their own everyday wear. We assess each home rather than assuming a village cottage and a newer house nearby need identical treatment.
