Ranelagh

A village high street with a higher-spec housing stock

Ranelagh sits just south of the Grand Canal, with the Luas Green Line running through the village centre and a genuine café and restaurant culture along the main street. It's one of Dublin's most recognisable "village" suburbs — settled, well-off, and less studenty than neighbouring Rathmines, even though rental turnover here is still high.

The housing is mostly Victorian and Edwardian red-brick terraces, and a noticeable number of them have been restored to a higher spec than comparable streets in Rathmines — original floors and fireplaces kept, finishes upgraded around them. Some of these houses are subdivided into apartments, generally finished well rather than thrown together, and there's a scattering of boutique new-build infill among the older terraces too. The result is a mix of well-off owner-occupiers and higher-income professional renters who tend to expect — and look after — a better finish than average, which shapes how we approach carpets, rugs and upholstery here.

That combination — older, better-restored houses plus a still-substantial rental market — means we see two fairly distinct jobs in Ranelagh. One is the long-term owner or period-house buyer looking after original floor coverings, stair runners and a rug or two worth taking care of properly. The other is the professional tenant or landlord managing a well-presented let, where the carpet needs to come up to a standard that matches the rest of the finish, not just be "clean enough." Both get the same hot water extraction method, adjusted to what's actually on the floor.

Services in Ranelagh

What we clean, and how it's different here

Carpet cleaning for Ranelagh's period terraces

Original stair carpets and runners in restored Victorian and Edwardian terraces need a more careful approach than a modern fitted carpet — checking the fibre, testing an inconspicuous area, and keeping moisture levels appropriate for older backing and underlay. In newer infill and subdivided apartments, carpets tend to be more recent and straightforward to clean. See our carpet cleaning Dublin page for how a standard visit works.

Upholstery & sofa cleaning

Ranelagh's well-kept interiors tend to come with a better-quality three-piece suite or sofa than average, and we treat fabric-safe cleaning accordingly — matching the method to natural or synthetic fibre rather than a one-size approach. Full detail on our upholstery cleaning Dublin page.

Mattress cleaning

A deep clean that lifts dust and everyday build-up out of the mattress rather than just the surface — useful ahead of a change of tenant, a house move, or simply as part of a periodic clean. See mattress cleaning Dublin.

Rug cleaning — delicate and higher-value pieces

Period-house ownership in Ranelagh tends to come with better rugs — wool, oriental or otherwise more valuable pieces that deserve a gentler, more considered clean than a routine carpet. We assess the construction and fibre before deciding on a method. See our rug cleaning Dublin page.

Stain & pet stain removal

Wine, coffee and everyday marks treated as part of a full clean or on their own, plus pet stain and odour treatment for the dog- and cat-owning households in and around the village. On a lighter-coloured or higher-spec carpet, a stain shows up faster and matters more — worth flagging early rather than waiting for the next full clean. See stain removal and pet stain removal.

Rental turnover

End of tenancy carpet cleaning in Ranelagh

Rental density in Ranelagh is still high, but the tenant profile leans more towards settled professional renters than the primarily student turnover found in some neighbouring areas — which tends to mean a higher expectation of finish at hand-back, not just a faster one. Whether you're a tenant protecting a deposit, a landlord between lets, or a letting agent coordinating a move-out, we carry out a carpet-focused clean timed for check-out, with a written invoice suitable for a deposit dispute or an agent's records.

This is the carpet component of an end-of-tenancy clean specifically, not a full-property clean — see our Dublin-wide end of tenancy carpet cleaning page for what that covers, or our dedicated end of tenancy carpet cleaning Ranelagh page for area-specific detail.

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Ranelagh

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Ranelagh?

We're renovating a period house in Ranelagh — can carpets be cleaned before contractors or movers arrive?

Yes. We can time a clean for just before or after building work, a renovation, or a house move, and we're used to working around trades and tight schedules in Ranelagh's period terraces. Let us know the timeline when you request a quote and we'll work around it where we can.
