# Camera Roll Review — Batch 4

**Source list:** `kk_chunks/kk_chunk_4` (195 filenames)
**Images opened:** 127 (~65% of batch)
**Images selected:** 5

## Notes

This batch was dominated by personal and non-carpet-cleaning business content: a large
proportion of the photos turned out to be from a house-clearance/removals side of the
business (antique furniture valuations, van loads of junk, storage units), plus family
photos, food, plants, vehicle dashboards/mechanical repairs, mobility-scooter servicing,
and general phone-roll clutter. Very few frames actually depicted carpet, upholstery,
mattress, or rug cleaning work. The picks below are the only genuinely usable, on-topic,
non-duplicate shots found after opening the large majority of the batch (including
sampling every burst of near-identical timestamps).

## Selected Photos

1. **20250603_124225.jpg** — Close, well-lit detail shot of a freshly cleaned dark
   charcoal-grey stair-runner carpet, showing clean pile texture and the stairwell
   handrail; good "after" texture shot for a stairs carpet cleaning gallery.
2. **20250603_124258.jpg** — Wide shot of a full staircase with the same dark
   charcoal-grey carpet fully cleaned, handrail and fire extinguisher visible for scale
   and context; strong "after" hero shot for a stairs/carpet cleaning page.
3. **20250818_122935.jpg** — Wide "before" shot of a heavily soiled beige/grey bedroom
   carpet with visible dark stains and scattered debris — good dramatic-soiling example.
4. **20250818_122943.jpg** — Closer detail shot of the same soiled carpet near the
   skirting board, showing multiple distinct stains — pairs with #3 as a before set.
5. **20250611_141453.jpg** — Mauve/purple sofa cushion with a visible dark stain on the
   armrest, in a living-room setting — usable as an upholstery-cleaning "before" /
   problem-illustration shot.
