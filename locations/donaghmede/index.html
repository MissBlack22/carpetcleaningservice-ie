Local to Donaghmede

Carpet cleaning built around Donaghmede's homes

Donaghmede is a settled, family-oriented northside suburb built up mainly through the 1970s and 80s, a little later than the 1950s–60s housing that dominates neighbouring Artane and Coolock. It's a small but genuine distinction: carpets here are somewhat less likely to be original to the house or decades old, and the area's well-established local shopping centre gives it its own sense of place rather than reading as an extension of its neighbours.

Most jobs are whole-house cleans for family homes — the kind of everyday wear that comes from kids, pets and busy households, rather than end-of-tenancy turnaround work, which is a much bigger part of the market in the rental-dense suburbs closer to the canal.
