Local to Castleknock

Carpet cleaning built around Castleknock's homes

Castleknock is a West Dublin suburb in D15, sitting directly against the eastern edge of Phoenix Park and neighbouring Blanchardstown. It's home to Castleknock College and Castleknock Golf Club, and is served by its own railway station on the commuter rail line, putting it firmly on the route out towards the Maynooth and Sligo direction. It's generally considered one of the more settled, affluent parts of the county.

The area itself is a mix of an older village core, with housing that's been established for longer, and later suburban estates built up around it as Castleknock grew. That mix means the carpet we're asked to clean varies more than in a single-era suburb — some of it long-installed in the older houses near the village, some of it newer fitted carpet in more recently built estate homes — so we check condition and fibre before recommending anything rather than assuming one approach fits every job.
