Local to Whitehall

A quiet link between two bigger northside hubs

Whitehall is a quiet, mostly mid-20th-century residential suburb on the northside, sitting between Glasnevin and Drumcondra on one side and Santry and Beaumont on the other. It's honestly more of a settled residential link between those larger, better-known neighbours than an area with a strongly distinct identity of its own — and we'd rather say that plainly than invent a character that isn't there.

What is genuinely true of Whitehall is a modest rental pocket tied to nearby Dublin City University, a feature it shares with Glasnevin and Ballymun rather than something unique to Whitehall itself, sitting alongside a larger base of settled, long-term family homes.
