FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Grangemore

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Grangemore?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and access — there's no fixed rate we can quote without seeing the details. See our carpet cleaning prices page, or request a free quote above and we'll come back with a clear price.

Is Grangemore covered as part of your Naas-area service?

Yes — Grangemore is covered as part of the wider Naas area we work in regularly, alongside Sallins, Johnstown and Kill. Because it's a smaller address without its own town centre, it's worth confirming your exact location with us before booking, which we're happy to do.

Do you clean carpets and rugs on site?

Carpets and fitted flooring are always cleaned in place, in your home. Rugs are usually cleaned on site too, though for delicate or heavily soiled pieces we may suggest collecting them for a more thorough clean — we'll agree the best option with you first.

What's included when you come out for a clean?

A visit typically includes assessing the carpet, treating any stains beforehand, and a full hot water extraction clean of the rooms you've booked, with a check-over before we leave. You can add upholstery or mattress cleaning to the same visit — just mention it when requesting your quote.
