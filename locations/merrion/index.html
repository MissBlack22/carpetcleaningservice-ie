FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Merrion

Is an original carpet in a Merrion period home worth cleaning, or should it be replaced?

It depends on the fibre and condition, which is exactly why we check before recommending anything. Plenty of the carpet we clean in Merrion's older homes responds well to a proper clean, and where wear has genuinely gone past that point, we'll say so rather than take the job anyway.

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Merrion?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and any furniture or access considerations — there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for a full breakdown, or request a free quote for a clear price before booking.

Do you cover Sandymount and Booterstown as well as Merrion?

Yes — Sandymount, Booterstown and the surrounding south Dublin coast are all part of the same patch as Merrion. If you're not sure whether your road is covered, get in touch and we'll confirm.

Does being close to Merrion Strand affect how carpets are cleaned?

Mainly through drying time — homes this close to the coast can hold more ambient moisture, so we're careful not to over-wet the carpet and we factor in realistic drying times, especially for older properties.
