Real commercial job — office floor

Built for business premises

Carpet cleaning that fits around how your business runs

Office and commercial carpet gets a different kind of wear to a home — heavier footfall, entrance-matting grime, and no room for a clean that puts the working day on hold. We plan each visit with that in mind.

Commercial premises typically use carpet tile as well as broadloom carpet, and both need to be handled correctly — different pile construction, different backing, different response to moisture. We use hot water extraction cleaning suited to the flooring in front of us, and we'll flag anything that looks like it needs a different approach before we start, rather than after.

Because most enquiries are different — a single office versus a multi-floor building, a retail unit with a small footprint versus a large open-plan space — quotes for commercial work are based on the actual premises: square footage, current condition, access, and whether you're after a one-off clean or an ongoing schedule. Send us the details and we'll come back with a price that reflects the job, not a generic rate card.

One-off commercial clean

A single deep clean for an office, retail unit or other premises — ahead of a client visit, after a refurbishment, or simply because it's overdue. Hot water extraction cleaning suited to commercial-grade carpet tiles and broadloom.

Scheduled & recurring cleaning

For businesses that want carpets looked after on an ongoing basis, we can put together a recurring schedule — monthly, quarterly or another interval that fits your footfall and budget — so cleaning stays a routine, not a fire drill.

How we minimise disruption

Working around your business, not against it

  1. 1

    Tell us about the premises and constraints

    Opening hours, footfall, areas that can't be closed off, and any timing pressure — the more we know upfront, the better we can plan around it.

  2. 2

    Out-of-hours or weekend scheduling can be arranged

    If a daytime clean isn't practical, evening, early-morning or weekend visits can be arranged so the space is ready before the next working day.

  3. 3

    Section-by-section cleaning where needed

    For premises that can't close entirely, we can work through zones in sequence, keeping part of the space usable while another section is cleaned and drying.

  4. 4

    Realistic drying time before you reopen

    We'll give you a practical estimate of drying time on the day, so you know when the space is genuinely ready for staff, customers or clients.

FAQ

Common questions from facilities & office managers

Can you work outside normal business hours?

Out-of-hours and weekend scheduling can be arranged for offices and businesses that can't have carpets cleaned during opening hours. Let us know your constraints when you request a quote and we'll work out a time that suits.

Do you offer scheduled or contract cleaning, not just one-off jobs?

Yes. Some businesses need a single deep clean, others prefer a regular schedule — monthly, quarterly, or whatever interval keeps carpets looking presentable between cleans. Tell us what you're after and we'll put together a plan.

What types of commercial premises do you clean?

Offices, reception areas, meeting rooms, retail units, and other business premises with carpeted floors across Dublin. If you're not sure whether your premises is a fit, get in touch and describe the space.

Will cleaning disrupt our working day?

We plan the visit around your business, not the other way round — moving light furniture where needed, working section by section where continuous access matters, and giving you a realistic drying estimate so you know when the space is back in full use.

Do you quote for multiple sites or ongoing contracts?

Yes — for multi-site businesses or facilities managers looking after several premises, get in touch with the details and we'll put together a quote that covers the full picture.
