Local to Artane

Carpet cleaning built around Artane's homes

Artane grew up mostly across the 1930s to 1960s as semi-detached and terraced housing spread out around what became a well-established local shopping centre, on the northside east of Beaumont and Coolock. It's a settled, largely owner-occupier suburb rather than a high-turnover rental area, and that includes Kilmore, which we treat as part of the same patch alongside Artane itself.

That long settled history means the housing stock here is now genuinely ageing — a lot of what we're asked to clean is original carpet or carpet that's been down for decades, in homes where the same family has stayed put. It also means the "clean or replace" question comes up more honestly here than in newer suburbs, and homes of this era more often had open fireplaces, so a faint smoke or soot residue in older carpet is a real, if occasional, part of the job — not something we'd claim for every house, but a genuine feature of housing this age.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Artane

Can professional cleaning remove old smoke or soot smell from an older carpet?

Often, yes. A lot of Artane's housing dates from the 1930s to 1960s, an era when open fireplaces were far more common, and some of the older carpet we're asked to look at has picked up a faint smoke or soot residue over the years. Hot water extraction lifts embedded residue out of the fibre rather than just masking it on top, though the result depends on how deep the residue has settled — we'll assess it honestly before quoting.

Is a carpet original to a 1960s house worth cleaning, or should it be replaced?

It depends on the fibre and condition, which is exactly why we check before recommending anything. Plenty of the carpet we clean in Artane's semis and terraces has been in place for decades, and a well-maintained older carpet often responds well to a proper clean and doesn't need replacing. Where wear has genuinely gone past the point cleaning can fix, we'll say so rather than take the job anyway.

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Artane?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and any furniture or access considerations — there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for a full breakdown of what affects cost, or request a free quote and we'll come back with a clear price before booking anything.

Do you cover Kilmore as well as Artane?

Yes — Kilmore sits right alongside Artane and we treat it as part of the same patch, along with the streets around the local shopping centre and out towards Coolock. If you're not sure whether your road is covered, get in touch and we'll confirm.
