Local to Arklow

Carpet cleaning built around Arklow's homes

Arklow sits where the River Avoca meets the sea in south County Wicklow, and its history as a working maritime town — boat-building, fishing and for much of the 20th century the Arklow Pottery works — has left a town centre with a real mix of older terraced housing near the harbour and river alongside newer estates further out. As the largest town in south Wicklow, it also serves as a hub for the surrounding villages up the Avoca valley.

That mix means the carpet we're asked to clean varies from older stair carpet in a harbourside terrace to more modern fitted carpet in a newer estate. Properties close to the river and harbour also deal with a bit more moisture in the air than those further from the water, which is worth factoring into how a carpet is cleaned and dried.

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Arklow

Do you cover homes near Arklow harbour and the River Avoca?

Yes. Arklow sits at the mouth of the River Avoca, and homes close to the harbour and riverside deal with a bit more airborne moisture than properties further from the water. Hot water extraction is dried out properly rather than left to sit, so it works well for these homes too.

Do you clean carpets in older houses near Arklow town centre?

Yes. Arklow has a long maritime history as a boat-building and fishing town, and some of the terraced housing near the town centre dates back decades, occasionally with original stair carpet still in place. We check fibre and condition before recommending anything, rather than assuming a standard clean is right for every carpet.
