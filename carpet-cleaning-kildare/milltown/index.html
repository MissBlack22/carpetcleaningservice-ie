How much does carpet cleaning cost in Milltown?

Do you cover the areas around Milltown as well?

Yes — we treat Milltown as part of the wider area we cover around Naas, including Kilcullen, Kilmeage and Sallins. If you're not sure whether your area is covered, get in touch and we'll confirm.

Are rugs cleaned in place or collected?

It depends on the rug. Some can be cleaned safely in place on the floor; others, particularly older or more delicate pieces, are better collected and cleaned off-site where we have more control over the process. We'll advise honestly once we've seen the rug.

A typical visit covers a pre-inspection of the carpet, pre-treatment of any spots or heavier soiling, and a full hot water extraction clean, followed by grooming the pile and leaving the carpet to dry. We'll talk you through anything specific to your home before we start.
