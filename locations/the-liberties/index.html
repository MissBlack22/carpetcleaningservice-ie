Local to The Liberties

Carpet cleaning built around The Liberties' inner-city mix

The Liberties is one of Dublin's oldest neighbourhoods, a dense inner-city area near the Guinness Storehouse where renovated period terraces, social housing and new-build apartment and regeneration schemes all sit close together. Renter density here is very high, and because the area is genuinely tourist-visited, that renter population includes a real short-let and Airbnb-style accommodation market alongside standard longer-term tenancies — not something that applies in most of the suburbs we cover.

That mix means the work varies more here than in a typical single-character suburb: long-term inner-city families in older buildings, young professional renters in converted or new-build apartments, and short-let hosts who need a quick, reliable clean turned around between guests.

Why it matters here

Why professional cleaning matters in The Liberties specifically

Short-let and tourist-facing apartments

Proximity to the Guinness Storehouse means a genuine short-let and Airbnb-style sub-market here, on top of standard rental turnover — a fast, dependable changeover clean matters more in The Liberties than in most other areas we cover.

Renovated period buildings

Older terraces here have often been renovated to a high standard, but what's underneath a new surface finish can vary — we check the fibre and underlay before treating it rather than assuming it's all been replaced.

Very high renter density

Alongside the short-let market, The Liberties has one of the highest general renter densities on our coverage list, so standard end-of-tenancy carpet cleaning for landlords and letting agents is a regular part of the work here too.

Dense, mixed housing stock

Social housing, renovated terraces and new-build apartment schemes sit within a few streets of each other, so we're genuinely equally set up for a period-house job and a modern apartment job on the same day.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in The Liberties

Do you offer fast-turnaround cleans for short-let or Airbnb-style apartments in The Liberties?

Yes. Given the number of short-let and tourist-facing apartments in The Liberties, close to the Guinness Storehouse, we're used to working to a guest-changeover schedule rather than an open-ended booking window. Let us know your turnaround time when you get in touch and we'll work around it.

Can you clean carpets in an older, renovated building in the area?

Yes. The Liberties has a genuine mix of older renovated terraces alongside newer apartment and regeneration schemes, and we're equally comfortable in both. In an older renovated building we'll check what's underneath the surface finish before we start, since a renovation doesn't always mean new underlay.

We're a landlord with a rental in The Liberties — do you do standard end-of-tenancy cleans as well as short-let turnarounds?

Yes, both. The Liberties has a high renter density generally, so alongside short-let turnaround work we do plenty of standard end-of-tenancy carpet cleaning for landlords and letting agents between longer-term tenancies.

How much does carpet cleaning cost in The Liberties?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and any furniture or access considerations — there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for a full breakdown of what affects cost, or request a free quote and we'll come back with a clear price before booking anything.
