Local to Lusk

Carpet cleaning built around Lusk

Lusk is a village and growing town in north County Dublin, in the Fingal area between Rush, Skerries and Swords. It has a genuine historic core, marked by the well-known round tower at its centre, and has expanded over recent decades with newer housing estates around the older village.

That combination of an old village centre and newer residential growth means we see a real range of carpet — from long-established homes near the village to more recently fitted carpet in newer estates. We assess each job on what's actually in front of us rather than assuming a single pattern.
