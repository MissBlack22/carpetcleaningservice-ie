Local to Kildare town

Carpet cleaning built around Kildare town's homes

Kildare town is one of the county's most historic towns, built up around St Brigid's Cathedral, and sits at the heart of Ireland's horse-racing and breeding country — home to the Irish National Stud and close to the Curragh Racecourse. In more recent years it's also become known for Kildare Village, which draws visitors well beyond the county.

Housing ranges from the older streets around the cathedral and Market Square to newer estates on the edges of town. We check fibre, underlay and condition before recommending anything — and where a household is connected to the local equine industry, that can mean a bit more mud, hay or straw finding its way onto carpets than in a typical home.

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Kildare town

Does Kildare town's horse-country setting mean different cleaning needs?

It can, in a general sense. Kildare town sits at the heart of Ireland's horse-racing and breeding country, close to the Curragh and the Irish National Stud, and households connected to the equine industry sometimes track in more mud, hay or straw than a typical home.
