Rathmines

A rental market unlike most of Dublin

Rathmines is one of Dublin's most recognisable neighbourhood names, and one of its most rented: a dense southside inner suburb just south of the Grand Canal, built around a busy village-scale main street. The housing stock is largely red-brick Victorian and Edwardian terraces, and a large share of that stock has been converted into flats, apartments and bedsits — alongside some larger period houses that remain single-family, and newer apartment blocks along the main roads.

That mix matters for carpet condition. Many converted flats went over to hard flooring at some point, so where carpet does survive it's often on communal stairs and landings shared by several households, taking far heavier footfall than a carpet in a single-family home. Combine that with a genuinely transient population of students and young professionals moving through short lets, and Rathmines produces a volume and pace of turnover cleaning that quieter, more settled suburbs simply don't.

What we clean

Cleaning built around how Rathmines is actually lived in

Carpet cleaning

Fitted carpets and stair runners in the period terraces, plus the shared stair carpets that run through Rathmines' converted houses — cleaned as the separate, heavier-use job they are, not lumped in with a single flat's clean. In smaller flats and bedsits we work efficiently around tight furniture layouts and narrow stairwells common to subdivided Victorian houses. See our carpet cleaning page for the full method.

Upholstery & sofa cleaning

Rathmines' apartments and shared houses see a lot of furnished lets, and a furnished sofa gets used by every tenant who passes through it — not just one household over years. Fabric-safe cleaning brings furnished-let upholstery back up to a standard worth showing the next tenant. Details on upholstery and sofa cleaning.

Mattress cleaning

Furnished student and professional lets typically include the mattress, which means it changes hands between tenancies just like the carpet does. A proper clean between lets deals with everyday build-up and allergens rather than just a change of sheets. More on mattress cleaning.

Rug cleaning

Where flats have gone over to hard flooring, rugs often do the job carpet used to — and in Rathmines' smaller rooms, a rug is frequently the main soft furnishing tenants and owner-occupiers actually own. We clean rugs in place or collected, matched to the fibre. See rug cleaning.

Stain & pet-stain removal

House-shares and short lets bring the usual run of everyday marks, and pets are increasingly part of the mix even in smaller Rathmines flats. Targeted treatment for wine, food and pet stains, on its own or as part of a full clean — see stain removal and pet stain and odour removal.

End of tenancy

End of tenancy carpet cleaning in Rathmines

Rathmines is the strongest end-of-tenancy market we cover. With this much rental density, tenancies turning over means carpets, communal stairs and furnished rooms need to be cleaned and ready against a fixed handover date, not a loose timeframe — and landlords and letting agents here are often managing that process for more than one unit at a time, sometimes in the same building.

We work directly with tenants trying to satisfy a deposit return, and with landlords and letting agents who need a consistent standard and a fast turnaround between one tenancy ending and the next beginning. FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Rathmines

Can you turn a carpet clean around the same day before new tenants move in?

Often, yes. Rathmines has one of the fastest tenancy turnarounds we see, so we're used to working to a fixed handover date rather than a loose window. Tell us the date the new tenants are due and we'll do what we can to fit the clean in before it, including same-day where the diary allows — the earlier you get in touch, the more options we can offer.

Do you offer arrangements for landlords with several rental properties in Rathmines?

Yes. A number of landlords and letting agents in Rathmines have more than one unit in the same building or nearby, and we can work with you across all of them — coordinating access, keeping a consistent standard between units, and invoicing in a way that suits managing several properties rather than a single one-off job.

Our house is split into flats with a shared stair carpet — do you clean communal areas?

Yes. Rathmines has a lot of period houses converted into multiple flats, and the shared stair carpet in those buildings gets far heavier footfall than a carpet in a single-family home — it's a genuinely different job, cleaned and priced on its own terms. We can clean the communal stairs and landing alongside or separately from any individual unit.

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Rathmines?

Cost depends on the number of rooms, the size of the flat or house, and the condition of the carpet or rug — a one-bed apartment and a full period house are priced differently. See our carpet cleaning prices guide for what affects cost, or request a free quote above.

Do you clean carpets in Rathmines' period Victorian and Edwardian houses?

Yes. Many of Rathmines' red-brick terraces still have original or long-fitted carpets and stair runners, whether the house remains a single family home or has been converted into flats. We check the fibre and test an inconspicuous area first, and adjust moisture and heat accordingly rather than treating it like a modern fitted carpet.
