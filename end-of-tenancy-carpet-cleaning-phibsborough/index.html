Why Phibsborough

A high-turnover rental market with a shift-working population

Phibsborough is a high-density northside inner suburb of red-brick Victorian terraces, similar in era and rental character to Drumcondra and Rathmines, sitting between the Mater Hospital, the Royal Canal and a Luas stop. A large share of the housing stock has been converted into flats and rooms, and rental turnover here is consistently high — established enough that, like Drumcondra, we treat it as a genuine end-of-tenancy hotspot rather than a general location-page section.

One thing that's a little different here: proximity to the Mater Hospital means a good number of tenants are shift workers on rotating or unsocial hours, which can make a standard daytime-only booking window awkward. Where that's the case for a tenant, landlord or agent arranging a move-out clean, we can talk through scheduling around it rather than assuming a nine-to-five slot works for everyone.

As with the other rental-dense northside and southside areas we cover, a lot of Phibsborough's period houses are subdivided into flats with a shared stair carpet, which we clean as its own job given the heavier use it gets compared with a single household's floor.

FAQ

Common questions about end of tenancy carpet cleaning in Phibsborough

Can you offer flexible booking times for hospital shift workers renting in Phibsborough?

We'll do what we can — a lot of Phibsborough's tenants work rotating shifts given how close the area is to the Mater Hospital, and we're used to being flexible about timing where a strict nine-to-five window doesn't suit. Mention it when you book and we'll see what's possible.

Do you clean shared stair carpets in Phibsborough's converted period houses?

Yes. Like Drumcondra and Rathmines, a lot of Phibsborough's terraces have been split into flats, and we clean the shared stair and landing carpet as its own job, separate from or alongside an individual unit's end of tenancy clean.

Can a letting agent book on behalf of a landlord for a Phibsborough property?

Yes. We work directly with letting agents and landlords in Phibsborough, including booking a clean between tenancies rather than waiting on the outgoing tenant to arrange it.
