Local to The Coombe

Carpet cleaning built around The Coombe's homes

The Coombe sits at the heart of the Liberties in Dublin 8, one of the city's oldest inner-city neighbourhoods, historically associated with weaving and the textile trade and still home to The Coombe Women & Infants University Hospital. The streetscape here is a genuine mix — older Victorian and Georgian-era terraced housing alongside a good number of newer apartment developments that have gone up in recent years as the area has changed.

That mix means the work varies a lot, from original floorboards and older carpet in period terraces that need a careful, gentler approach, to newer fitted carpet and rugs in more recently built apartments. Given how central the area is, we also see a fair share of rental turnovers alongside long-term family homes.
