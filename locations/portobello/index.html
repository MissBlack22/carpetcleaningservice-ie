Local to Portobello

A canal-side rental market, close to Rathmines

Portobello sits along the Grand Canal around Portobello Harbour, a short walk from Rathmines and Ranelagh — red-brick terraces, much like Rathmines' housing stock, with a very high share converted into flats and shared houses. The area has a genuinely distinct history too, having been known for decades as Dublin's "Little Jerusalem", the heart of the city's Jewish community. Today it's one of the most rental-dense pockets on the southside, smaller and more tightly bordered by the canal than Rathmines, but with the same student and young-professional tenant base driving demand.

That density means most of the jobs we're called out for in Portobello are tied to tenancies changing hands rather than long-settled family homes — carpets and stair carpets that need to be brought up to standard between one tenant leaving and the next arriving.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Portobello

Do you offer the same fast end-of-tenancy turnaround in Portobello as in Rathmines?

Yes. Portobello has the same kind of rental density and tenancy turnover we see in neighbouring Rathmines, just across a smaller area, so we work to the same fixed handover dates and fast-turnaround standard. See our dedicated end of tenancy carpet cleaning Portobello page for details specific to this area.

What happens if I need the carpet cleaned before my landlord's inspection?

Tell us the inspection or handover date when you get in touch and we'll work to it. Deposit-related cleans are a routine part of what we do in Portobello, so we're used to booking around a fixed date rather than a loose window.

Do you clean carpets in Portobello's older canal-side terraces as well as flats?

Yes. Alongside the rental flats, Portobello still has red-brick terraces with original or long-fitted carpet and stair runners, and we treat those with the same care as any period property elsewhere on the southside.
