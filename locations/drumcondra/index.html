Drumcondra

A northside suburb shaped by rental density and Croke Park

Drumcondra is a high-density northside inner suburb close to both Dublin City University and Croke Park, Ireland's largest stadium. The housing stock is mostly red-brick Victorian and Edwardian terraces, similar in era and density to Rathmines and Phibsborough, with a high rate of conversion into flats and shared houses. That means a genuinely large student and young-professional rental market alongside family-sized terraces on the quieter streets further from the main roads.

What makes Drumcondra distinct is Croke Park itself: on top of the standard high tenancy turnover it shares with Rathmines and Phibsborough, Drumcondra has a real seasonal micro-market in match-day changeover cleans for short-let properties near the stadium. It's worth naming directly rather than folding into generic end-of-tenancy copy, because it genuinely changes how some bookings here are timed. The area's coverage also extends into Ballybough, the small inner-city pocket linking Drumcondra with North Strand, which shares much of the same terraced housing stock and rental pattern.

What we clean

Cleaning built around how Drumcondra is actually lived in

Carpet cleaning

Fitted carpets and stair runners in the period terraces, plus the shared stair carpets that run through Drumcondra's subdivided houses — cleaned as the heavier-use job they genuinely are, not lumped in with a single flat's clean. In smaller flats we work efficiently around tight furniture layouts and narrow stairwells common to converted Victorian houses. See our carpet cleaning page for the full method.

Upholstery & sofa cleaning

Furnished student and professional lets are common here, and a furnished sofa gets used by every tenant who passes through it, not just one household over years. Fabric-safe cleaning brings furnished-let upholstery back up to a standard worth showing the next tenant. Details on upholstery and sofa cleaning.

Mattress cleaning

Furnished lets near DCU and Croke Park typically include the mattress, which changes hands between tenancies just like the carpet does. A proper clean between lets deals with everyday build-up rather than just a change of sheets. More on mattress cleaning.

Rug cleaning

Where flats have moved to hard flooring, rugs often do the job carpet used to, and in Drumcondra's smaller rooms a rug is frequently the main soft furnishing a tenant or owner-occupier actually owns. We clean rugs in place or collected, matched to the fibre. See rug cleaning.

Stain & pet-stain removal

House-shares and short lets bring the usual run of everyday marks, and match-day weekends can add their own wear and tear to a short-let property. Targeted treatment for wine, food and pet stains, on its own or as part of a full clean — see stain removal and pet stain and odour removal.

End of tenancy

End of tenancy carpet cleaning in Drumcondra

With this much rental density near DCU, tenancies turning over means carpets, communal stairs and furnished rooms need to be cleaned and ready against a fixed handover date, not a loose timeframe. We work directly with tenants trying to satisfy a deposit return, and with landlords and letting agents who need a consistent standard and a fast turnaround between one booking and the next. FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Drumcondra

Can you do a fast turnaround clean around a big match weekend for a short-let property near Croke Park?

We can try to accommodate this where our schedule allows. Drumcondra's proximity to Croke Park means some short-let and Airbnb-style properties in the area see a seasonal spike in bookings around big match weekends, with a tight changeover window between guests. Get in touch as early as possible with your dates and we'll do what we can to fit the clean in around them.

Do you clean shared stair carpets in subdivided period houses in Drumcondra?

Yes. Drumcondra has a lot of red-brick Victorian and Edwardian terraces converted into flats, similar in era and density to Rathmines and Phibsborough, and the shared stair carpet in those buildings takes far heavier footfall than a carpet in a single-family home. We clean communal stairs and landings alongside or separately from any individual unit.

Do you offer end of tenancy carpet cleaning for landlords with multiple properties in Drumcondra?

Yes. Drumcondra has a high rate of rental conversion, and a number of the landlords and letting agents we work with here manage more than one unit in the same building or nearby.
