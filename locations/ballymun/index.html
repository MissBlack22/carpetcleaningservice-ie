Local to Ballymun

Carpet cleaning built around Ballymun's homes

Ballymun looks quite different from most of the areas we cover. The regeneration that replaced the area's older tower blocks with newer housing and apartments through the 2000s left a genuinely younger housing stock — a mix of social and private housing, with some accommodation linked to nearby Dublin City University, on the northside above Glasnevin and Santry.

That newer housing changes what "needs cleaning" actually looks like here. Tile and laminate are standard as flooring in a lot of Ballymun's living areas and kitchens, with carpet more often limited to bedrooms than fitted right through a house the way it typically is in the city's older suburbs. So we don't assume a whole-house carpet job by default in Ballymun — the honest starting point is usually bedroom carpets, rugs, upholstery and mattresses, and we're just as happy to book any one of those on its own.

Why it matters here

Why the service mix looks different in Ballymun

A younger housing stock

Regenerated housing means less of the decades-old original carpet common in older northside suburbs — condition and soiling here tend to look different, room by room.

Carpet limited to bedrooms

Hard flooring is standard in a lot of Ballymun's living areas, so bedroom-carpet cleaning is often the more relevant job than a full-house carpet clean.

Rugs on hard floors

Open-plan living rooms with tile or laminate mean rugs are a common furnishing choice here — we assess the fibre and clean them properly, not as an afterthought.

A DCU-adjacent household mix

Some student and staff accommodation near Dublin City University sits alongside family housing, so the jobs we see range from family bedrooms to smaller rented units.

Rug cleaning

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Ballymun

Do you clean carpets in newer apartments as well as houses?

Yes. Ballymun's housing is generally newer than most of the areas we cover, following the regeneration that replaced the area's older tower blocks with newer housing and apartments, so we're used to working in modern apartment layouts as well as houses — including where carpet is limited to bedrooms rather than fitted throughout.

Is upholstery or mattress cleaning worth booking if I don't have carpet throughout my home?

Yes, and it's a genuinely common booking in Ballymun for exactly that reason. With hard flooring standard in a lot of the newer housing here, sofas, mattresses and rugs often do more of the day-to-day wear than a hallway or living-room carpet would elsewhere, so cleaning them on their own is a normal, standalone job rather than something that only makes sense alongside a carpet clean.

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Ballymun?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and any furniture or access considerations — there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for a full breakdown of what affects cost, or request a free quote and we'll come back with a clear price before booking anything.

Do you cover areas near DCU in Ballymun?

Yes — Ballymun sits close to Dublin City University and we cover the accommodation and housing around it as part of our normal service area. Get in touch if you're not sure whether your address is included.
