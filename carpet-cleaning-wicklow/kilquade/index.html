FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Kilquade

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Kilquade?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and access, so there's no single flat rate we can give upfront. See our carpet cleaning prices page for what affects cost, or request a free quote above and we'll come back with a clear price before booking anything.

Do you cover Kilquade as well as Kilpedder?

Yes — Kilquade sits right beside Kilpedder and we cover both as part of the same patch, along with Greystones and Newtownmountkennedy nearby. If you're unsure whether your road is included, get in touch and we'll confirm.

Can a rug be cleaned in place, or does it need to be taken away?

It depends on the fibre and construction. Many rugs can be cleaned in place alongside the carpet, but some are better collected for off-site cleaning — we'll check and let you know before starting.

What's included when you clean a carpet?

A visit typically includes checking the carpet and agreeing scope, pre-treating any marked areas, a full hot water extraction clean, and guidance on drying time afterwards. We'll confirm the exact details for your home when quoting.
