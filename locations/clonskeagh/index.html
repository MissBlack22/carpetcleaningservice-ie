Local to Clonskeagh

Carpet cleaning built around Clonskeagh's homes

Clonskeagh sits between Ranelagh and Donnybrook on the southside, close to UCD's Belfield campus — a leafy, mostly residential stretch of period homes and low-rise apartment development along the main road. It shares Donnybrook's proximity to the university, but on a smaller scale: the rental pocket here is real but modest, and Clonskeagh reads as quieter and more purely residential than its more media-and-institution-adjacent neighbour. We also treat Milltown, the small area folded in between Clonskeagh and Ranelagh, as part of this same patch.

That mix — settled owner-occupier homes alongside a smaller student and postgraduate rental market tied to UCD — means the work here splits fairly evenly between whole-house family cleans and rental turnaround jobs timed around term dates, rather than one dominating the other.

What we clean

Carpet, upholstery, mattress and rug cleaning in Clonskeagh

Most jobs are either a full carpet clean in a family home on the quieter residential roads, or a turnaround clean in a smaller rental or apartment let to postgraduate students or staff connected to UCD — hot water extraction handles both, lifting ground-in dirt without over-wetting. See carpet cleaning Dublin for the method. Upholstery cleaning covers sofas and armchairs in longer-owned period homes as well as furnished rental lets; mattress cleaning is a common add-on wherever the equipment's already on site. Where a rug is involved — increasingly likely in the area's period houses — we assess the fibre first rather than treating every rug the same way, and stains and everyday marks are treated at the source, not just covered up.
