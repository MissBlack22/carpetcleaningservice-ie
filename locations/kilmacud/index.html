Local to Kilmacud

Carpet cleaning built around Kilmacud's homes

Kilmacud is a residential suburb in South Dublin, sitting between Stillorgan, Dundrum and Goatstown, and home to Kilmacud Crokes, one of the country's best-known GAA clubs. It's a settled, mostly owner-occupier area with housing largely dating from the mid-to-late twentieth century.

With a strong local sporting community and long-established families, a lot of the homes we work in around Kilmacud have been lived in for years, and the carpet, rugs and upholstery we're asked to clean reflect that settled character rather than a quick-turnover rental market.
