# Camera Roll Review — Batch 1

**Source chunk:** `kk_chunks/kk_chunk_1` (195 filenames, Dec 2024 – Feb 2025 date range)
**Images opened:** ~155 of 195 (roughly 80%), sampling 1–2 frames per burst of near-identical shots and opening all singles.
**Images selected:** 1

## Findings

This date range of the camera roll turned out to be almost entirely non-work content: house clearance / junk removal jobs (furniture, boxes, rugs being cleared out), a "For Sale" estate agent sign photographed at a job site, medicine boxes and pharmacy receipts, a Deliveroo tablet, Sonos speaker teardown/cables, cough pastilles, a barber shop haircut, family meals, a bronze figurine/ornaments, an attic full of storage boxes, and two photos of the Krystal Klean business card itself (not usable as a photo, just the card design). None of these are usable as real job photography for the website.

Only one image in the entire batch showed genuine carpet/upholstery work documentation.

## Selected image

1. **20250106_141942.jpg** — Close-up "before" shot of a beige sisal-style stair carpet runner on a landing, with a visible faint water/soil stain in the pile. Clean, sharp, well-lit close-up with no people in frame. Useful as a "before" or stain-documentation shot; a matching "after" was not found in this batch. (A near-duplicate frame, 20250106_141945.jpg, was taken 3 seconds later from a slightly wider angle — 141942 was kept as it's the tighter, more usable crop.)

## Note for follow-up

Given how few usable photos this chunk contained, it may be worth checking whether other date ranges (chunks) in the camera roll have denser job-photo coverage — this range appears to be mostly personal/errand photos rather than on-site cleaning documentation.
