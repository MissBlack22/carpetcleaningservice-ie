Carpet cleaning built around Kill

Kill is a village on the N7 in northeast County Kildare, between Naas and the Dublin border. It's a well-established village with a mix of older housing around the centre and newer estates on its edges.

FAQ

Common questions about carpet cleaning in Kill

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Kill?

It depends on the number of rooms, carpet size, condition and access — there's no single flat rate. See our carpet cleaning prices page for a full breakdown, or request a free quote above.

Do you cover Naas as well as Kill?

Yes — Kill sits right on the N7 close to Naas and we cover both as part of the same regular route, along with Johnstown and Rathcoffey nearby. Get in touch if you're not sure your exact address is covered.

Are rugs cleaned in the house or collected?

Most rugs can be cleaned in place, on the floor where they sit. For delicate or heavily soiled rugs we may suggest collecting them for a more thorough off-site clean — we'll agree the right approach with you before we start.

What's included in a carpet cleaning visit?

A visit covers an assessment of the carpet, pre-treatment of any stains, and a full hot water extraction clean of the agreed rooms, followed by a check before we leave. Mattress or upholstery cleaning can be added to the same visit if you'd like.
